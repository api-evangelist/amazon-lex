Condé Nast's editorial teams spent an average of 250 minutes per task searching a library of more than 140,000 videos using only titles and descriptions. Working with the AWS Generative AI Innovation Center, they built a multimodal video discovery solution on Amazon Bedrock and Amazon OpenSearch Service that cut discovery time to under 2 minutes.
